The tracked CSV's 858-row, 36-column structure appears consistent with the UCI Machine Learning Repository's **Cervical Cancer (Risk Factors)** dataset. UCI credits Kelwin Fernandes, Jaime Cardoso, and Jessica Fernandes (2017), DOI [10.24432/C5Z310](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5Z310), and lists the dataset under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
