# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Cervical-Cancer-Prediction project welcomes contributors who treat one
another with respect. Health data and medical claims require particular care:
discussion should remain evidence-focused, privacy-conscious, and free of
personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive.
- Discuss evidence, assumptions, and code rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and corrections from contributors at different experience
  levels.
- Protect the privacy of patients, dataset subjects, and contributors.
- Keep criticism proportionate and focused on the work.
- State uncertainty honestly, especially for health-related results.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, personal attacks,
unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and disclosure of private information
without consent are not acceptable. Do not use health status, identity, or
personal history to target or shame someone.

Do not publish patient-level records or sensitive notebook output to make a
point. Do not encourage others to use an unvalidated model on real patients.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
spaces where someone is representing this project. It also applies to conduct
outside those spaces when it materially affects another person's ability to
participate.

## Reporting

Contact Brajesh31 privately through the GitHub profile to report a concern.
Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Include the project space,
approximate time, a concise description, and any context needed to review it.
Share only information that is safe to disclose.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review reports as promptly and fairly as practical,
protect privacy where possible, and consider relevant context from everyone
involved. Responses may include a warning, removal of harmful content,
temporary participation limits, or exclusion from project spaces.

Retaliation against reporters or participants in a review is not acceptable.
This project is maintained on a best-effort basis; response timing is not
guaranteed.
## Good-faith disagreement

Differences about data cleaning, statistics, model choice, or interpretation
are not misconduct by themselves. Explain your reasoning and avoid attributing
bad motives to another contributor.
## Privacy in project spaces

Do not include health records, personal identifiers, private messages, or
sensitive details in public discussion. Use synthetic examples and share
private context only through a suitable channel.
## Evidence and uncertainty

Be clear about whether a statement is measured, inferred, or hypothetical.
Do not present the notebook's metrics as medical evidence or use them to
pressure another person into a clinical choice.
