# Ethics, responsible research, and governance

This repository is an educational analysis of a historical dataset. Public availability does not automatically authorize every reuse, upload, publication, or clinical application.

## Responsibilities
1. Verify provenance, license, and transformation history.
2. Define the research question and avoid overclaiming what retrospective data can show.
3. Minimize access to patient-level data.
4. Protect notebook outputs, temporary files, logs, and backups.
5. Assess bias, missingness, representation, and dataset context.
6. Avoid stigmatizing descriptions of sexual, reproductive, or health-related attributes.
7. Report uncertainty and do not provide individual medical recommendations.
8. Obtain appropriate expert and institutional review for work beyond education.

## Medical language
Do not claim the notebook diagnoses cancer, detects cancer early, determines individual risk, or recommends screening. The current predictors include diagnosis and screening fields, prediction timing is undefined, and the analysis is not clinically validated.

## Data reuse
Confirm source and applicable license before redistribution. Keep attribution and record transformations. A code license does not replace data rights or ethics obligations.

## Privacy
Do not publish records, rare combinations, or screenshots of notebook outputs. Use synthetic examples. Assess transfer, retention, access, jurisdiction, and contractual controls before using hosted notebooks or external tools.

## Future capabilities
Any app, API, upload feature, or data collection proposal needs a defined purpose, lawful authority, risk assessment, data minimization, access controls, encryption, retention/deletion, auditability, incident response, and accountable operators.

## Review questions
- Is the use permitted by source terms and provenance?
- Is the research question appropriately scoped?
- Are features available at the intended prediction time?
- Could outputs mislead or harm a person?
- Are uncertainty and limitations visible?
- Is the environment approved for sensitive data?
- Has required expert and ethics review been obtained?

When these questions are unresolved, do not expand the use case.
