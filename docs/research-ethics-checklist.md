# Research ethics and responsible-use checklist

Use this checklist before a new analysis, dataset change, public report, or proposal that expands the project's scope.

## Research purpose
- [ ] The question is clear and proportionate to the data.
- [ ] The work is described as educational or research analysis, not a diagnostic service.
- [ ] The target population and prediction time are explicit.
- [ ] The intended and excluded uses are documented.

## Data rights and provenance
- [ ] The source dataset is verified as far as possible.
- [ ] The license and attribution obligations are understood.
- [ ] Transformations and exclusions are recorded.
- [ ] Permission for any added or redistributed data is documented.

## Privacy
- [ ] Access is limited to an approved environment.
- [ ] No direct identifiers or patient-level values appear in public materials.
- [ ] Notebook outputs, charts, logs, and exports are reviewed.
- [ ] External service transfers and retention are assessed.

## Scientific quality
- [ ] Missingness and data quality are reviewed.
- [ ] Feature availability and target leakage are audited.
- [ ] Split and preprocessing avoid leakage.
- [ ] Metrics represent both positive and negative classes.
- [ ] Uncertainty and external-validity limits are stated.

## Harm and fairness
- [ ] Dataset representation and subgroup limits are understood.
- [ ] Potential false-positive and false-negative harms are discussed.
- [ ] Language is non-stigmatizing and does not blame individuals.
- [ ] Results cannot reasonably be mistaken for individual advice.

## Review
- [ ] Appropriate domain, clinical, privacy, and ethics experts have reviewed the proposal where required.
- [ ] The README safety warning remains prominent.
- [ ] The decision and unresolved issues are recorded.

Completion of this checklist is not clinical or regulatory approval.
